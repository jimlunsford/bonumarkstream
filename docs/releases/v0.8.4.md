## Recovery and acceptance

Before deployment, require tested recovery material containing the actual installation's files, database, owner data, custom themes, and dependencies. Rehearse the exact packaged ZIP on an isolated restoration. Disable outbound delivery and scheduled publishing before launching the clone, and document rehearsal-only changes.

Before migration begins, use the upgrader's changed-software recovery behavior. Once migration may have begun, preserve target-version files and resume the same exact ZIP through the documented forward-recovery workflow. An older-file overlay over a changed schema is not a safe rollback. Full files-and-database restoration must use a consistent paired recovery point and explicitly account for writes lost since that point.

Package validity and compatibility CI do not establish deployment readiness. Current-target inspection, owner-data preservation, recovery rehearsal, and desktop/mobile browser acceptance remain separate gates. The Work 40 review evidence records their observed results and any blockers.
